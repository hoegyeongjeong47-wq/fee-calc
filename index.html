How Etsy Seller Fees Work in 2026

Pricing handmade, vintage, or digital products on Etsy requires understanding every fee deducted from your order total. Many new sellers only account for the $0.20 listing fee and are surprised when transaction, payment processing, and shipping-related fees eat into their profit margins.

Below is the complete breakdown of standard Etsy fees for US-based shops using Etsy Payments.

Fee Type Standard Rate (US) When It Applies
Listing Fee $0.20 per item Charged when you publish a listing and automatically renewed for $0.20 each time a unit sells.
Transaction Fee 6.5% of total price Applied to the item price, shipping charged to the buyer, and gift wrapping combined.
Payment Processing Fee 3.0% + $0.25 Charged on the total order value (including shipping and applicable sales tax) when using Etsy Payments.
Offsite Ads Fee 15% or 12% Only charged if a buyer clicks an external Etsy ad (Google, Meta, Pinterest) and buys within 30 days.

Step-by-Step Etsy Profit Calculation Example

Suppose you sell a custom ceramic mug for $35.00 and charge the buyer $5.00 for shipping. Your total gross revenue is $40.00. Your materials cost $10.00, actual postage costs $4.50, and packaging costs $1.00 (total costs = $15.50).

  • Listing Fee: $0.20
  • Transaction Fee (6.5% of $40.00): $2.60
  • Payment Processing Fee (3% of $40.00 + $0.25): $1.45
  • Total Etsy Fees: $4.25 (10.6% of total revenue)
  • Net Profit: $40.00 − $15.50 − $4.25 = $20.25 (50.6% profit margin)

Practical Ways to Protect Your Etsy Profit Margins

Because Etsy charges its 6.5% transaction fee on both the item price and the shipping fee, shifting costs from the item price to shipping does not lower your fees. Instead, consider these strategies:

  • Bundle low-priced items: The fixed $0.20 listing fee and $0.25 payment fee take a massive percentage out of $5–$10 items. Selling multi-packs raises your average order value (AOV) and lowers your effective fee rate.
  • Build a buffer for Offsite Ads: If your shop crosses $10,000 in sales over a 12-month period, Offsite Ads become mandatory at 12%. Adding a 2–3% pricing buffer across all listings prevents sudden margin shocks.
  • Track true packaging and label costs: Tape, mailers, thermal labels, and bubble wrap typically add $0.50 to $1.50 per order. Always include them in your calculator inputs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Etsy charge fees on shipping?

Yes. Both the 6.5% transaction fee and the payment processing fee (3% + $0.25 in the US) are calculated on the combined total of the item price and the shipping amount charged to the buyer.

Can I opt out of Etsy Offsite Ads?

If your shop has earned less than $10,000 USD in the past 365 days, Offsite Ads are optional (15% fee) and you can turn them off in your Shop Manager settings. Once you exceed $10,000 in 12 months, participation becomes mandatory for the lifetime of the shop at a discounted 12% rate.

Are Etsy fees refunded if I cancel an order?

When you issue a full refund and cancel an order, Etsy credits back your transaction fee and payment processing fee. However, the original $0.20 listing fee is generally non-refundable unless the entire order is canceled under specific conditions.

What is a good profit margin for an Etsy shop?

Most profitable handmade and print-on-demand Etsy shops target a net profit margin between 30% and 50% after all fees, materials, and shipping costs. Digital product sellers often achieve 75% to 85% margins due to zero physical production or shipping expenses.
