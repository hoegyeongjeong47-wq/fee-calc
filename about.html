About SellerCalc

SellerCalc is an independent web resource built to help online sellers, freelancers, and small business owners accurately calculate platform fees, payment processing charges, and true net profit margins.

Online marketplaces frequently update their fee structures, making it easy for sellers to underprice products and lose margin on shipping or payment processing. Our goal is to provide fast, free, and transparent calculators that run directly in your browser without requiring logins or subscriptions.
