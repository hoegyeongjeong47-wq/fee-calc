How eBay Seller Fees Are Calculated

eBay deducts fees from the total order amount paid by the buyer—which includes the item price, shipping charges, handling, and applicable taxes or import fees. Rather than collecting separate payment processing fees outside eBay, managed payments bundle standard selling costs into the final value fee plus a small per-order surcharge.

Fee Component Typical Structure Notes
Final Value Fee ~12.35% – 14.95% (varies by category) Calculated on total order amount; tiered percentages may apply above certain dollar thresholds in select categories.
Per-Order Fixed Fee $0.30 (≤ $10) or $0.40 (> $10) Charged once per completed buyer order, even if the buyer purchases multiple items in a single order.
Promoted Listings Seller-set ad percentage Applies if the buyer clicks your promoted ad and purchases within eBay's attribution window.

Tips to Keep eBay Margins Healthy

  • Factor shipping into total order fees: Because eBay's percentage fee applies to the total amount collected from the buyer, offering "free shipping" by rolling postage into the item price does not increase your percentage fee—but undercharging shipping directly reduces your net profit.
  • Watch ad rates on thin-margin items: A 5%–10% promoted ad rate on a low-margin item can wipe out profit quickly. Test ad percentages gradually and track return on ad spend (ROAS).
  • Combine multiple items into one order when possible: The fixed per-order fee ($0.30 or $0.40) is charged per order rather than per line item.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does eBay charge final value fees on shipping?

Yes. eBay calculates its final value percentage on the full order total paid by the buyer, including shipping and handling charges.

Why is there a per-order fee in addition to the percentage fee?

eBay charges a flat per-order fee ($0.30 for orders of $10 or less, and $0.40 for orders over $10) to cover payment processing and transaction handling per order.

Are eBay fees refunded if an order is canceled or refunded?

When a seller issues a refund or cancels a transaction properly, eBay generally credits back the corresponding final value fee and per-order fee (minus specific non-refundable adjustments depending on the case).
