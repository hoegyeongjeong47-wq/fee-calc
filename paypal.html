PayPal Merchant Fee Rates (US, 2026)

PayPal charges a percentage fee plus a fixed fee ($0.49 for USD transactions) whenever you receive commercial payments. Understanding which rate applies helps freelancers and e-commerce sellers price invoices accurately.

How Reverse PayPal Fee Calculation Works

If you want to receive an exact net payout (for example, $100.00 after fees), simply adding 2.99% + $0.49 is not enough because PayPal also charges its percentage fee on the extra amount you added. The exact reverse formula is:

Amount to Request = (Desired Net Amount + Fixed Fee) ÷ (1 − Percentage Rate)

For a $100.00 net payout at 2.99% + $0.49, you should request ($100 + $0.49) ÷ 0.9701 = $103.59.
